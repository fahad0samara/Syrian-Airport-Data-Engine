# Final Source Evidence Audit — Damascus International Airport (DAM / OSDI)

**Audit Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Audit Scope:** 100% individual inspection of all 180 DAM flight events currently classified as completed movements (`landed` or `departed`).  
**Audit Method:** Record-by-record verification against primary raw payloads, Flightradar24 terrestrial ADS-B radar telemetry, official airport authority portal schedules, and pairwise deduplication audit logs.  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Reconciliations

An exhaustive record-level audit was conducted across every completed Damascus flight event in `data/damascus_airport.db` (`WHERE airport_id = "DAM" AND flight_status IN ("landed", "departed")`).

### Key Findings & Final Verdict Totals

- **Total Audited Completed Events:** **180**
- **Verified Physical Movements (`verified`):** **98** (48 arrivals, 50 departures)
- **Records Requiring Review (`needs_review`):** **79** (41 arrivals, 38 departures)
- **Unsupported Operations (`unsupported`):** **3** (0 arrivals, 3 departures)

### Verdict Categories Defined

1. **`verified` (98 events):** Physical movement at Damascus International Airport (OSDI/DAM) confirmed by ground terrestrial ADS-B radar telemetry (`real.arrival` wheels-down for arrivals, `real.departure` wheels-off for departures). Unambiguous UTC timestamps derived directly from unix epoch seconds. Includes 75 cross-validated events merged with official Damascus airport schedules (`damairport_official+flightradar24`) and 23 verified solo radar flights (`flightradar24`). Zero physical duplicates exist within this verified set.
2. **`needs_review` (79 events):** Records containing operational data but disqualified from direct inclusion in physical movement counts due to duplication, codeshare status, or timestamp representation artifacts:
   - **54 Direct Physical Duplicates:** Operating carrier flights that duplicate an already-verified Flightradar24 event. These were kept separate during ingestion because AviationStack reported local Damascus wall-clock time with a `+00:00` offset string (e.g. `10:02:00+00:00` instead of `07:02:00Z`), resulting in a 180-minute discrepancy against UTC radar observations.
   - **19 Marketing Codeshares:** Commercial flights (e.g. Emirates EK on flydubai, Qatar Airways QR on Royal Jordanian, Saudia SV on flyadeal, Oman Air WY on Royal Jordanian) sharing physical aircraft with operating flights. Counting them would artificially double-count movements.
   - **6 Solo AviationStack Records:** Records lacking independent terrestrial ADS-B radar touchdown/takeoff confirmation and carrying uncalibrated timezone strings.
3. **`unsupported` (3 events):** Schedule-only departure records (`RB515`, `RJ436`, `WY5232` on 2026-10-10) where AviationStack copied `scheduled` departure time into `actual` departure time (`dep.actual == dep.scheduled`). These records lack genuine measured wheels-off operational evidence at DAM. The actual physical departures for the operating flights were already verified at earlier timestamps by Flightradar24.

## 4. Reconciliation of the 98 vs. 121 Discrepancy

A central question addressed by this audit is why the rebuilt database contains **121 completed events** while the verified ground-movement count is **98**.

### Mathematical & Operational Reconciliation

$$\text{Total Completed DAM Events (121)} = \text{Verified Ground Movements (98)} + \text{Marketing Codeshares (20)} + \text{Official+AS Merged Solo Flights (2)} + \text{IATA Variation (1)}$$

1. **98 Independently Verified Ground Movements (`verified`):**
   - 48 arrivals (`landed`) and 50 departures (`departed`).
   - Backed 100% by Flightradar24 terrestrial ADS-B radar ground telemetry (`time.real.arrival` wheels-down, `time.real.departure` wheels-off) at Damascus International Airport.
   - 71 are triple-merged (`damairport_official+aviationstack+flightradar24`), 23 are dual-merged (`aviationstack+flightradar24`), and 4 are dual-merged (`damairport_official+flightradar24`).
   - **All 98 previously verified movements still exist in the database with identical timestamps and dates.**

2. **20 Marketing Codeshares (`needs_review` / `unsupported`):**
   - Commercial flights (`EK2179/2180`, `QR6118/6119`, `WY5231/5232/5233/5234`, `SV4037/4038/4055/4056`, `GF5931`) where AviationStack raw payload explicitly identifies the operating carrier in `flight.codeshared`.
   - Because their flight numbers are distinct marketing identifiers, they are not merged into operating carrier flights, preserving commercial inventory fidelity.
   - 19 are classified as `needs_review` (commercial codeshare duplicates of operating flights).
   - 1 (`WY5232` on 2026-10-10) is classified as `unsupported` because its departure timestamp was copied from scheduled time (`actual == scheduled == '08:40:00+00:00'`) without measured takeoff telemetry.

3. **2 Official Portal + AviationStack Solo Movements (`needs_review`):**
   - `RJ439` (arrival on 2026-10-09 at 02:51 UTC) and `RJ440` (departure on 2026-10-09 at 04:28 UTC).
   - These flights merged between Damascus Official Portal schedule and AviationStack operational feed, but lack Flightradar24 terrestrial ground telemetry.

4. **1 IATA Code Variation (`needs_review`):**
   - `DN486` (Dan Air IATA code for Fly Cham `FYC486`). AviationStack recorded this under `DN` rather than `FYC`.

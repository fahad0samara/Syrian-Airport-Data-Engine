## 1. Executive Summary & Semantic Methodology

To prevent invalid assumptions regarding data attributes, Version 2.14 evaluates every field in the 1,982 flight objects extracted from the `2026-07-09T23:42:30Z` Internet Archive snapshot.

Field semantics were determined not merely from field names, but through exhaustive value inspection, cross-referencing against the API's top-level `settings` and `airlines` metadata dictionaries, and astronomical/timezone verification.

## 3. Critical Evidentiary Interpretations

### 3.1 Total Absence of Physical Movement Timestamps
A critical finding of the field inspection is that **zero transponder or radar timestamp fields exist in the payload**:
- There is no `actual_time`, `actual_arr_time`, `actual_dep_time`, or `runway_time`.
- The `time` field represents strictly the **scheduled departure or arrival time** published for passengers.
- The `createdAt` field represents the CMS row insertion timestamp when airport staff uploaded the timetable, not a sensor detection of an aircraft touching down.

### 3.2 Analysis of the Single "arrived" Record
Exactly one record out of 1,982 has `status: "arrived"`:
```json
{
  "id": "a3c520d5-a113-4646-bdff-c60c2e8e27b9",
  "flightNumber": "XH  728",
  "airline": "فلاي شام",
  "direction": "arrival",
  "origin": "DXB",
  "destination": "DAM",
  "date": "2026-07-06",
  "time": "00:20",
  "gate": "",
  "aircraft": "",
  "status": "arrived",
  "createdAt": "2026-07-06T00:27:33Z"
}
```
#### Evidentiary Rules Applied:
1. The record is classified as `archived_actual_movement_candidate` to reflect its raw status label.
2. In strict accordance with Section 5 of the specification:
   > *"Do not infer actual takeoff or landing from: a past scheduled date, a flight number, a populated scheduled time, or an on-time label without actual movement evidence. Even then, do not promote the record to production physical_movement without a separate evidence review."*
3. Because `time: "00:20"` is the scheduled time and `createdAt: "2026-07-06T00:27:33Z"` is a CMS database timestamp, this record **contains zero physical transponder ground hits**.
4. **Conclusion**: It remains in the staging layer and is **strictly prohibited from being promoted to a verified physical movement** in `flight_events`.

## 4. Normalization and Timezone Conversions

Damascus operates on Damascus Local Time (`UTC+3` year-round under Syrian permanent daylight saving time).

For every record in `archived_fids_normalized`:
- `scheduled_datetime_local` is constructed as: `{date}T{time}:00+03:00`
- `scheduled_datetime_utc` is calculated by subtracting exactly 3 hours:
  * Example: Local `2026-07-06 00:20` converts to UTC `2026-07-05T21:20:00Z` (with proper calendar day rollover).
